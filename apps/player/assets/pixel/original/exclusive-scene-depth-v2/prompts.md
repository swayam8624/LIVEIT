# Art direction prompts — exclusive scene depth v2

These are the exact internal prompts used to guide the authored studies. They are retained as design provenance; the final pixels are deterministic Pillow primitives in `build_layers.py`.

## Shared direction

“Original 2D retro pixel art for a warm life simulator. Use a compact 160×128 design grid, crisp nearest-neighbour clusters, restrained dark ink outlines, readable silhouettes at phone size, and a palette of dusk blue, cream, coral, mint, lavender, and warm gold. Compose the scene as independent background, middle-ground, foreground, guide, and prop layers. Keep the centre lane clear for two actors and dialogue. No logos, existing characters, copied sprites, photorealism, 3D geometry, or third-party imagery.”

## Graduation

“An open-sky school graduation at sunset: deep blue sky, tiny stars, coral horizon stripe, dark school roof silhouette, gold school emblem, stage riser, two banners, benches, a diploma table, and curtain-and-foliage framing. The mood is hopeful and earned. Separate diploma scroll, graduation cap, wildflower bouquet, and class medal props.”

## New parenthood

“A small lived-in home at first light: two windows, a pale wood floor, a mint bassinet, a low side table, a soft lamp, and quiet red-and-gold curtain accents. The mood is tender and sleep-deprived but safe. Separate swaddled baby, family photo, keepsake rattle, and welcome-home card props.”

## Elder legacy / birthday

“A memory room at night: deep blue walls, framed photographs, a long table with small warm lights, a central keepsake display, and dark curtain framing. The mood is grateful, reflective, and intergenerational. Separate memory album, garden seed packet, old key, and legacy letter props.”

## Negative direction

“Do not flatten actors into the background, do not place text in the actor lane, do not use copyrighted characters, game screenshots, recognizable brands, archive art, realistic 3D, glossy vector gradients, or hard-to-remove opaque props.”
