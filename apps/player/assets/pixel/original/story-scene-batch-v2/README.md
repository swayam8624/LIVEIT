Three original pixel-art flat scene concepts for life milestones and exclusive story moments.

## Panels

- Bellweather bookshop opening: an adult bookseller welcomes a friend in warm evening light.
- Town garden graduation: teen friends and family celebrate at sunset.
- Family elder birthday: an older woman celebrates with multiple generations at home.

Each panel is 724×724 RGB from one equal-width triptych. Dialogue-safe area coordinates are advisory metadata, not validated UI guarantees. `story-triptych-concept-master.png` preserves the generated source. `scenes.json` records scene crops, hashes, provenance, and non-runtime status.
