# Family portrait expressions v1

Original pixel-only bust concepts for the named Bellweather family: Mara, Eli,
Niko, and Aunt Sol. The batch provides twelve everyday, non-romantic acting
states for each character:

`neutral`, `happy`, `sad`, `angry`, `shocked`, `scared`, `smug`, `crying`,
`embarrassed`, `tired`, `thoughtful`, and `speaking`.

## Files and layout

- `frames/<character>/<character>-<emotion>-96x96.png` — 48 transparent RGBA
  bust frames, authored at 48×48 and enlarged to 96×96 with nearest-neighbour
  sampling.
- `family-portrait-expressions-atlas-12x4-96x96.png` — exact-output atlas;
  columns follow the emotion order above and rows are Mara, Eli, Niko, Aunt Sol.
- `family-portrait-expressions-contact-2x.png` — 2× review contact sheet.
- `manifest.json` — stable IDs, identity notes, pivots, per-frame SHA-256
  hashes, source-reference hashes, and `runtimeReady: false` declarations.
- `build_family_portraits.py` — deterministic Pillow builder.

Every frame uses a 48,92 pivot inside its 96×96 cell. These are concept assets,
not admitted runtime sprites: opaque bounds, emotional readability, palette
consistency, collision use, and renderer sampling still need human review.

## Identity and safety

The existing local [named-family concept batch](../named-family-batch-v1/README.md)
was used only as an identity and palette reference. Its board and character
metadata are recorded in `manifest.json` with SHA-256 hashes. The builder does
not sample or copy those images, and it reads no ZIP, web, or third-party art.

Mara remains the deep-brown-skinned adult parent with short curls, rust
cardigan, and mustard scarf. Eli remains the olive-skinned adult parent with
close-cropped hair, round glasses, navy jacket, and ochre shirt. Niko remains a
school-age child with a cobalt hoodie and orange hair tie. Aunt Sol remains an
older relative with a silver braid, plum coat, sage accent, and gold jewelry.
Niko's states are ordinary child acting only; `embarrassed` is a small blush and
`smug` is playful pride, with no romance or adult-coded intimacy.

## Provenance

All output pixels are generated by `build_family_portraits.py` from original
primitive geometry and a limited authored palette on 2026-10-20. No source ZIP,
external image, game-art reference, logo, or generated image was copied into
the output. The manifest is the integrity record for this revision.
