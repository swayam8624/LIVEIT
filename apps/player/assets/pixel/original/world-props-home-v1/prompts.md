# Prompt record

The source sheet was generated as one original concept-art request so all props
share a palette and outline language. No supplied ZIP, third-party sprite, or
internet image was used as a visual input.

```text
Create a clean, original, modular 2D pixel-art home-interior prop sheet with
distinct isolated props arranged in a regular 4-by-3 grid on a transparent
background: single bed, two-seat sofa, small dining table with two chairs,
kitchen counter with sink and upper cabinet, compact refrigerator, bathtub with
shower, baby crib, wooden storage chest, tall wardrobe, floor lamp, potted plant,
and wall clock. Use consistent dark plum-brown outlines, warm cream highlights,
teal and coral accents, clear transparent gutters, no labels, characters,
background, watermark, or overlapping objects. Crisp intentional pixel clusters,
limited palette, strong silhouettes, warm cozy indoor mood, original designs only.
Avoid photorealism, 3D rendering, vector gradients, and anti-aliased edges.
```

The requested transparency was preserved in the generated RGBA source. The
derived cells are uniformly 128x128 and bottom-aligned at pivot `(0.5, 1.0)`;
the source and derived images remain concept art pending manual pixel cleanup
and engine integration.
