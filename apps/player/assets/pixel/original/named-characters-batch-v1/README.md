# Named characters batch v1

This package adds original 24×32 RGBA movement concepts for five named actor
families in the historical pixel catalog: Amelia, Noah, the school teacher,
parent one (canonical Mara), and parent two (canonical Eli). It is deliberately
isolated under `assets/pixel/original/`; no runtime imports or catalog records
were changed.

## Coverage

Each family has 24 independently authored frames:

- four cardinal directions: `south`, `west`, `east`, `north`;
- four meaningful gait phases: `contact-a`, `passing-a`, `contact-b`,
  `passing-b`;
- `idle` and `interact` poses in every direction.

Every frame is an individual 24×32 RGBA PNG with a shared bottom-center pivot
at `(12, 31)`. Each character also has a six-column × four-row nearest-neighbor
atlas and a 4× review preview. `manifest.json` maps all 120 frames to the
exact historical catalog IDs and records SHA-256 hashes.

## Identity cues

Amelia preserves the existing brunette bob, cream blouse, teal apron accents,
and teal satchel. Parent one preserves canonical Mara's deep-brown skin,
short natural curls, rust cardigan, mustard scarf, pear-shaped silhouette, and
tote accent. Parent two preserves canonical Eli's olive skin, close-cropped
hair, navy work jacket, ochre tie/shirt accent, and round glasses. Noah is an
original tall café-supervisor identity with rolled sleeves, orange work shirt,
navy trousers, and an apron notebook. The teacher is an original compact blue
knit-sweater identity with a chalk badge and lesson pointer.

Cardinal views are drawn as separate primitive clusters. West and east are not
generated by flipping one another; asymmetric hair, prop, arm, and face pixels
are authored by direction. The interaction pose reaches toward a prop and is a
true silhouette change rather than a bob-only variation.

## Provenance and status

The PNGs and atlases are generated by the deterministic local Pillow builder
`build_assets.py`. It reads no user ZIPs, third-party sprites, external images,
or internet assets. The exact method and identity references are recorded in
`provenance.json`. This is original project-owned concept work; no third-party
license is asserted.

All manifest entries remain `runtimeReady: false`. Pixel cleanup, identity
continuity review against the older concept sheets, renderer import, palette
accessibility review, collision-box checks, and native visual QA remain before
promotion to runtime assets.

## Files

- `manifest.json` — exact catalog mappings, frame records, dimensions, pivots,
  hashes, and atlas metadata.
- `provenance.json` — generation method, identity references, and rights
  boundary.
- `frames/<character>/` — 24 individual frames, one folder per actor.
- `frames/<character>/<character>-movement-atlas.png` — 144×128 atlas.
- `frames/<character>/<character>-movement-preview-4x.png` — nearest-neighbor
  review image.
- `named-characters-contact-sheet-2x.png` — five-family review sheet.
