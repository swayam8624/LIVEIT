# Diverse youth cycles v1

This isolated concept batch adds two original identity studies:

- `child-mika`: a compact, springy child with round curls, a coral hoodie,
  mustard bottoms, and teal shoes.
- `teen-ravi`: a taller teen with side-swept auburn hair, a slate jacket,
  orange trousers, and an asymmetric messenger bag.

Each identity has 16 transparent 24x32 frames: south, west, east, and north
directions × contact-a, passing-a, contact-b, and passing-b phases. The profile
directions are authored independently; east and west are not generated by
mirroring one another. The shared pivot is `[12, 31]`.

`build_cycles.py` is the complete deterministic builder. It creates the frame
PNGs, 96x256 nearest-neighbor atlas, 384x1024 review preview, `manifest.json`,
and `provenance.json`. `identity-spec.json` preserves the palette and silhouette
inputs used by the builder. All frame, source-spec, and builder SHA-256 hashes
are recorded in the manifest.

This is concept art and is intentionally `runtimeReady: false`. It still needs
human pixel cleanup, palette approval, collision-box review, timing selection,
and renderer/import validation before it can enter the game asset pipeline.

No supplied ZIP contents, downloaded art, or third-party source artwork was
used as an input.
