# Foundation Sprint 001: A New Beginning

## Product promise

Free Life and Story Mode are equally important entrances to one deterministic model of people, resources, memories and consequences. A compatible encounter updates its current life; a standalone campaign owns its own continuity. No advertisements, waiting timers, paid meaningful choices or runtime generative AI.

## Playable scope

The player is 23 and moves to fictional Bellweather with £1,800, 72 wellbeing, 20 experience, Amelia (35 trust) and Noah (55 trust). All time is simulated; returning after a real-world absence has no penalty. Routine living costs £28 per day. Work earns £65 daily freelance or £120 employed; work costs 4 wellbeing and adds 1 experience. Study adds 4 experience and costs 1 wellbeing; rest adds 9 wellbeing; time with Noah adds 3 trust and 5 wellbeing. At 25 experience, a work action obtains steady employment. Bounded stats stay within 0–100. Untended relationships lose 1 trust at each weekly boundary after seven days without contact.

Activities advance 1, 7 or 30 days, applying each intervening day. The ledger records each activity interval and important weekly events. Money is integer pence; negative balances are currently permitted. Debt, bankruptcy, health crises and promotions are future rules, not implied working features.

## Story: The Space Between

Amelia waited through a year of silence. The player can apologize, listen, or respond defensively. Honesty leads to reconnection; taking things slowly begins a quieter repair; choosing more space leaves the distance intact. Seven scenes include three endings. Facts, memories, trust and a choice history retain the meaning of reconverging branches. The runtime supports fact and trust conditions even where a particular authored branch is unconditional.

Free Life launches this as a compatible encounter. Standalone Story Mode creates a new 23-year-old protagonist with the chosen identity in a separate timeline. A completed standalone timeline can continue in Free Life. Replaying replaces only the standalone slot. Starting a new life requires an explicit replacement action and replaces both slots.

## Acceptance

- Character creation affects the saved player identity.
- Activities advance time and change connected systems.
- Dialogue choices branch, change trust and record facts and memories.
- Those changes survive storage round trips and browser reloads.
- Standalone campaign progress resumes and remains isolated from the main life.
- Both apps use the same original content and validated schema.
- Creator Studio edits and exports local drafts with explicit validation errors.

## Boundaries

Publishing an encounter into the player, cloud saves, accounts, content discovery, moderation, asset ingestion, full lifespan simulation, localization, Skia rigging, audio and store releases are not implemented. The present narrative is shorter than the later 10–20-minute slice target. Native source and export compatibility do not establish real-device readiness.
