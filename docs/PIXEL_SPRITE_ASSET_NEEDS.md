## Minimum complete actor family

The game simulation distinguishes infant (0–<2), toddler (2–<5), child (5–<13), teen (13–<18), adult (18–<65) and senior (65+). Art production needs separate infant and toddler states rather than collapsing both into “child.”

For each important character, the target actor family is at least six ages × four directions × four locomotion frames, plus per-direction idle and interact poses (24 frames per stage, or 144 base frames across six simulation stages). Current direction-specific generic studies provide movement for eight age/presentation stages and idle/interact poses for seven. The linked named player family now has full walk cycles across infant-to-elder woman plus a young-adult/midlife-man movement branch leading toward the elder-man presentation variant. Separate batches now add young-adult, midlife, elder-woman, and elder-man idle/interact poses in all four directions. The young-adult/midlife adaptations are palette-matched but simplified rather than redrawn anatomy; human continuity reconciliation remains. Separate generation means exact identity continuity is not proven. Infant and toddler use crawling/reaching and waddling/reaching in place of an adult walk cycle. The original appearance kit is south-facing; two independent four-direction extensions now add different starter layer ranges and composite examples (see `appearance-directional-v1/` and `appearance-directional-v2/`). They use distinct pivots and palettes and are alternatives, not yet a unified renderer contract. Age fit, broader wardrobe variety, occlusion, and continuity across stages remain.

Named NPCs need independent silhouettes, palettes, outfits and faces; crowd archetypes can use smaller frame budgets. Mara, Eli, Niko, and Aunt Sol now each have isolated four-direction, four-phase walk-cycle concepts; these still need visual cleanup and runtime validation. Portraits should include neutral, happy, sad, angry, flirty (adult-only), shocked, scared, smug, crying, embarrassed, tired and thoughtful, plus dialogue mouth/blink/gaze timing. Minor characters must never receive adult-coded romance or intimacy expressions.

## Supplied archive rights triage

The archives were inspected in place; no unverified archive contents were extracted into the repository.

- **Commercial/public-repo redistribution documented:** Pulse Everyday Streets (CC0; a selected subset is already present); Kenney UI Pack, Pixel UI Pack, UI Audio and Music Jingles (CC0). These may be considered for targeted future needs after duplicate review and provenance capture.
- **Commercial use with attribution:** game-icons.net SVGs are CC BY 3.0 or individually CC0. Treat as attribution-required unless an exact icon's CC0 status is established; retain contributor-specific attribution.
- **Not suitable for public raw asset import:** SuperRetroWorld Character Pack permits commercial game bundling with credit but prohibits redistributing raw or modified source assets and prohibits AI use. Do not put its files in this repository or feed them to image generation.
- **Non-commercial-only:** Modern Interiors Free explicitly forbids commercial use; do not use in the game.
- **Unverified/no license in archive:** Four Skies, Owlish Media Sound Effects and Top-Down Retro Interior are inspiration-only until their exact source terms are independently confirmed.
- **Redistribution-restricted or terms needing separate legal review:** Humble Gift permits commercial use/modification but forbids sharing or resale of its original or modified assets. Do not place its raw files in this public repository.

No asset from these archives was used to generate the new age-arc artwork. Continue using the [intake pipeline](OPEN_SOURCE_ASSET_PIPELINE.md) for any future third-party import.
