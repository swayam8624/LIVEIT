# Working visual direction and asset standard

Baseline: quiet, mature, illustrated 2.5D. Warm paper, forest green, terracotta accents, generous space, expressive faces and cinematic natural light. Current native-primitives art is temporary and explicitly not production character artwork.

Explore three directions before locking production: (1) warm editorial illustration, currently prototyped; (2) painterly dusk with richer light and depth; (3) graphic storybook with stronger silhouettes and flatter colors. These are written art hypotheses, not completed generated concept sheets or an approved final art direction.

Proposed reusable asset contract: body rig ID and version; neutral front/three-quarter poses; normalized eye line and feet anchors; body, neck, head, hair-front, hair-back, clothing and hand layers; transparent PNG or vector source; expression variants for neutral, warm, uncertain, hurt and amused. Scene packs need background, midground, furniture, foreground and lighting layers, plus safe character zones for portrait and landscape framing. Keep text out of artwork. Maintain readable UI contrast independently of scene lighting.

Every production asset needs source, creator, license/usage evidence, generation provider where relevant, human edits, version, content hash and supported rig/style IDs. No production assets or licensing claims are supplied in this sprint. Prototype animation uses subtle translation; reduced-motion support and richer gestures are next presentation tasks.
